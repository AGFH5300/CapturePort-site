CapturePort product information

Field reporting for inspectors, engineers, surveyors and field service teams

CapturePort is an Android-focused mobile field reporting application. It is designed to help professionals collect photographs and observations at the job site, organize evidence and prepare structured client reports without rebuilding the story of the visit later.

Request pilot access

What problem does CapturePort address?

Inspection and service teams often gather images and notes on site, then spend additional time sorting them and composing a report. CapturePort is designed to keep relevant captures and notes together in a single project and support professional, editable reporting output.

Field reporting overview

Typical reporting workflow

  1. Create a project and enter relevant job, vessel or site identification.
  2. Capture site photographs and add observations while the details are fresh.
  3. Review, edit and arrange the findings for the audience receiving the report.
  4. Prepare a client-facing report in the formats supported by the available app version.

Offline field capture is a core design priority. Availability of particular export, login and licensing functions can differ between test releases and later commercial versions.

Questions about CapturePort?

Is CapturePort available to download?

Public downloads are not yet offered through this website. Check the official download page for current access information.

Can CapturePort replace a surveyor's technical judgment?

No. Inspection findings, accuracy and professional sign-off remain the responsibility of the person preparing the report.

Who should request pilot access?

Marine surveyors, superintendents, service engineers, property inspectors, loss adjusters, facilities teams and technicians who regularly create evidence-based field reports.
