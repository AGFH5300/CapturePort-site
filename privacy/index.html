Privacy Policy

1 Who we are

CapturePort is a mobile field reporting application developed and operated by CapturePort. Our primary domain is captureport.app. Contact us at support@captureport.app.

2 What data we collect

In the current version of CapturePort (v1), the app operates in fully local, offline-first mode. All data stays on your device:

  • Project data — project names, client names, vessel or site names, and job references you enter are stored only on your device.
  • Photos — photos you capture or import are copied into local app storage and are not transmitted anywhere.
  • Voice notes — voice recordings are stored locally on your device only.
  • Typed notes and captions — any text you enter is stored locally on your device.
  • Report drafts and exports — reports are generated entirely on your device. Exported files are saved locally and shared only if you choose to share them.
  • App settings — your report identity, branding fields, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

We do not collect, transmit, or store any of this data on external servers in the current version. If you email us at support@captureport.app, we receive only the information in that email, used solely to respond to you.

3 How data is stored

All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite (for project metadata, drafts, settings) and local device file storage (for photos, voice notes, and exported files). We do not use cloud storage or remote servers in the current version.

Because all data is stored on your device, its security depends on your device's own security settings (screen lock, encryption). We recommend keeping your device secured, especially if your reports contain sensitive information.

Using Reset Local App Data in Settings permanently deletes all project data, captures, drafts, and export history from the app. This cannot be undone.

4 How we use your data

Because all data remains on your device, CapturePort does not process your report content, photos, or project information for any purpose other than powering the features you use. We use locally stored data only to display your content in the app, generate report previews and export files on your device, and remember your settings between sessions.

5 What we do not do

  • We do not upload your photos, voice notes, reports, or project data to any server.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information or report content with third parties.
  • We do not use your report content for advertising, marketing, or model training.
  • We do not require you to create an account or log in to use the app.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use invasive device identifiers such as IMEI or device fingerprinting.
  • We do not use your private reports as public examples, templates, or SEO content.

6 Permissions (camera and microphone)

CapturePort requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera — used only when you actively take a photo within the app. We do not access your camera at any other time.
  • Microphone — used only when you actively start a voice note recording. We do not access your microphone at any other time.
  • Photo library — used only when you choose to import a photo from your device gallery. The selected photo is copied into local app storage.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your device Settings. This prevents the related feature from working but does not affect data already stored in the app.

7 Third-party services

In the current version (v1), there are no active third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud service integrations within the app. The app is built on Expo (React Native), and standard platform-level telemetry from Google (Android) may apply as part of the operating system — this is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Future versions may integrate cloud services such as authentication and cloud storage. This Privacy Policy will be updated before any such integrations are activated.

8 Data retention and deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, it is retained until you delete it, uninstall the app, or use the Reset function in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes the app's database and associated files from your device. We hold no copies of your data on any server in the current version, so no server-side deletion is required.

Questions about deletion? Contact us at support@captureport.app.

9 Children's privacy

CapturePort is designed for professional use by adults. The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us at support@captureport.app.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as we add new features such as cloud sync, user accounts, or billing. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date and notify users through the app where practicable. Continued use of CapturePort after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
