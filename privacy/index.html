Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This policy explains how CapturePort handles information during pilot and early access. We may update this policy as the product evolves.

Information we may collect

  • Account details such as name, email, company, and role.
  • Field content you upload, including photos, notes, and voice inputs.
  • Optional OCR output generated from images.
  • Optional location data when enabled by you.
  • Technical and usage logs for security, support, and reliability.

How we use information

  • To provide and improve CapturePort features.
  • To generate AI-assisted report drafts from your submitted inputs.
  • To respond to support, privacy, and service requests.

AI drafting notice

CapturePort can help prepare report drafts. AI-generated content may contain errors or omissions. You must review and approve all outputs before operational use or sharing.

Data retention

We retain data only as needed for legal, security, fraud-prevention, billing, operational, and support purposes, with retention periods based on applicable requirements.

Your requests

For support, privacy questions, or data deletion requests, contact support@captureport.app.
