Privacy Policy

Effective: 24 May 2026  ·  Last updated: 24 May 2026  ·  captureport.app

Plain English: CapturePort requires an account (email address) to use the app. Your projects, photos, voice notes, and reports are stored securely in the cloud so you can access them across devices. We use Sentry to monitor app stability — it collects crash logs and a device identifier. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the services listed in this policy.

You can request deletion of your data or your full account at any time — see Section 9.

1 Who we are

CapturePort is a mobile field reporting application developed and operated by CapturePort. Our primary domain is captureport.app. Contact us at support@captureport.app.

2 What data we collect

CapturePort collects the following categories of data:

Account data

  • Email address — required to create an account and log in via one-time password (OTP). Stored securely in our authentication system (Supabase).
  • Name — optional. Used to identify you on generated reports. Stored in your account profile.

Report and project data

  • Project data — project names, client names, vessel or site names, and job references you enter.
  • Photos — photos you capture or import during inspections.
  • Voice notes — voice recordings you create within the app.
  • Typed notes and captions — text you enter against captures or report sections.
  • Report drafts and exports — generated report files (PDF, Word) created from your captured data.
  • Company branding — your uploaded company logo or letterhead, if provided.

All report and project data is stored locally on your device while offline and synced to your secure cloud account when a connection is available.

App performance data

  • Crash logs — automatically collected when the app crashes, to help us diagnose and fix issues.
  • Diagnostics — app performance metrics collected automatically.
  • Device identifier — a device-level identifier used by our crash reporting system to group and analyse crash events.

Performance data is collected automatically and sent to Sentry (see Section 7). It does not include the content of your reports or captures.

3 How data is stored

CapturePort is currently in an early pilot stage and uses a local-first architecture:

  • On your device — all data is stored locally using SQLite and local file storage. The app is fully functional offline.
  • Cloud sync status — full production cloud sync is not generally available in the current MVP. If optional pilot sync is introduced for selected testers, it will be communicated separately.

Your local device security also matters. We recommend keeping your device secured with a screen lock, particularly if your reports contain sensitive commercial or technical information.

4 How we use your data

  • Contact data you submit — to respond to pilot applications, support requests, privacy requests, and account/data deletion requests.
  • Report and project data — to power the app's core features: capturing, organising, and generating inspection reports.
  • App performance data — to monitor app stability, diagnose crashes, and improve performance.
  • Email address — to reply to your support or pilot request. No marketing emails without your consent.

We do not use your report content, photos, or project data for any purpose beyond providing the service to you.

5 What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or report content with third parties for commercial purposes.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or marketing targeting.
  • We do not use your report content, photos, or captured data to train AI or machine learning models.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not use your private reports as examples, templates, or public content.
  • We do not share your data with any third party other than the services explicitly listed in Section 7.

6 Device permissions

CapturePort requests the following device permissions:

  • Camera — used only when you actively take a photo within the app.
  • Microphone — used only when you actively start a voice note recording.
  • Photo library — used only when you choose to import a photo from your device gallery.

Permissions are requested at the point they are needed, not on app launch. You can revoke any permission at any time in your device Settings. This will prevent the related feature from working but will not affect data already stored in the app.

7 Third-party services

CapturePort may use the following third-party services. Use depends on the pilot build you are using.

Supabase — Planned / Limited Pilot Infrastructure

Cloud authentication/sync is not generally available in the current MVP. If enabled for a limited pilot group, related processing will be limited to that pilot scope and communicated to participants. Supabase Privacy Policy →

Sentry — Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

Automatically collects crash logs, diagnostic data, and a device identifier when the app experiences an error. This data does not include your report content or personal information beyond the device identifier. Used solely to diagnose and fix app issues. Sentry Privacy Policy →

Google Play Testing / App Distribution Status

Standard platform-level telemetry from Google (Android) and Apple (iOS) may apply as part of the operating system and app store. This is governed by Google's and Apple's own privacy policies respectively.

8 Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data:

  • Where network features are used, data transmitted between the app and our services is encrypted in transit using TLS.
  • Data you keep only on your device remains under your device security controls.
  • Current MVP use may not require authentication for local-only use.
  • Access to your account data is restricted to you and authorised CapturePort personnel only.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you become aware of any security concern related to your account, please contact us immediately at support@captureport.app.

9 Data retention and deletion

In the current MVP, most user data is stored locally on your device. You have the following rights:

  • Delete specific data — request deletion of individual projects, reports, photos, or your company branding without closing your account. Submit a data deletion request →
  • Delete your account — request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data. We will complete this within 30 days of your confirmed request. Submit an account deletion request →

Pilot data note: Because data may be local-only in the current MVP, you are responsible for exporting and backing up important reports. CapturePort cannot recover reports lost due to device loss, reset, or uninstall unless cloud backup has been explicitly enabled for your pilot cohort.

To request deletion, email support@captureport.app from your registered address. We respond within 2 business days.

10 Children's privacy

CapturePort is designed for professional use by adults. The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us at support@captureport.app.

11 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as we add new features. When we make significant changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and notify users through the app where practicable. Continued use of CapturePort after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
