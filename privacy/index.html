Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 12, 2026

This policy explains how CapturePort handles information during our early pilot stage and product preparation. It may be updated before full public launch.

Information we may collect

  • Account information such as name, email, company, and role.
  • Field content you submit, including photos, notes, and voice inputs.
  • Optional OCR output extracted from images.
  • Optional location data when enabled by you.
  • Technical and usage logs needed for support, reliability, and security.

How we use information

  • To operate and improve CapturePort features.
  • To generate AI-assisted report drafts based on your submitted inputs.
  • To provide support, troubleshoot issues, and respond to requests.

AI drafting notice

CapturePort can assist in preparing report drafts. These outputs are generated from user-provided data and may include errors or omissions. Users are responsible for reviewing and approving all report content before use or sharing.

Data retention

We retain data for operational, support, security, and legal purposes, and only for as long as reasonably necessary for these purposes. Retention periods may vary based on account status and applicable requirements.
