Inspection Report App for Engineers and Inspectors

CapturePort is the offline-first mobile app that helps site engineers, MEP teams, snagging consultants, and equipment inspectors capture structured evidence on site and generate professional PDF and Word reports before they leave.

The inspection reporting problem

Most inspection reports are written hours after the inspection — from incomplete notes, uncaptioned photos, and fading memory. The result is weaker evidence, missed findings, and reports that take far longer than they should.

CapturePort is built to solve this at the point of capture. You caption each photo immediately after taking it, record observations on site, and generate a structured draft before you leave. By the time you get back to the office, the report is already 70% written.

The core difference: A photo captioned on site in 15 seconds — "Cracked render at first floor window lintel, south elevation" — is worth more than a photo described from memory 4 hours later. CapturePort makes on-site captioning the default.

How inspection reporting works with CapturePort

  1. Create a project — name the inspection, add site address, client, and job reference
  2. Capture on site — photos with captions, voice notes, and typed observations saved locally
  3. Review and reorder — swipe through captures, edit captions, sequence photos correctly
  4. Export — PDF for sharing, Word for editing. Photo appendix built automatically.

Frequently asked questions

Does CapturePort work without internet on site?

Yes, completely. CapturePort is offline-first by design. All captures and report drafts are saved to your device. You can export a full PDF or Word report without any internet connection.

How many photos can an inspection report contain?

There is no hard limit. Reports with 60–100 photos are common for site and snagging inspections. The app handles large photo sets cleanly with 1, 2, or 4 photos per page in the export.

Can supervisors edit the report after the inspector exports it?

Yes. The Word export is fully editable. The inspector exports the draft, shares it via email or WhatsApp, and the supervisor opens it in Word on their laptop to review, adjust, and produce the final version for the client.

Is CapturePort suitable for a snagging company with multiple inspectors?

Currently each inspector works independently on their own device. Team features — shared projects, company branding, and a web dashboard — are on the product roadmap. Join the pilot to be first to access them.
