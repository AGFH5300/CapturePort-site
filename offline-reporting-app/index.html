Offline Reporting App for Field Engineers

CapturePort works with zero internet connection — by design. Every capture, every photo, every report draft is saved locally on your device. No signal needed at any stage from capture to export.

Why offline-first matters for field reporting

Field professionals work in environments where connectivity is unreliable, slow, or completely absent. Engine rooms on vessels. Roof spaces and plant rooms. Remote construction sites. Underground facilities. Offshore platforms. Trying to run a cloud-based reporting tool in these environments creates friction at exactly the wrong moment — when you're on site and need to capture.

CapturePort is built differently. Everything happens on the device. There is no upload step, no sync required, no waiting for a connection. You capture, you export, you share — entirely offline if needed.

What offline-first means in CapturePort

  • No internet needed to start — open the app, create a project, and capture immediately
  • Photos saved locally — every photo is copied into secure local storage on the device
  • Voice notes saved locally — audio files saved to the device, never uploaded automatically
  • Report draft generated locally — the entire PDF or Word export happens on the device, no server call
  • Sharing is your choice — the report lives on your phone until you choose to share it via email, WhatsApp, or any app
  • Cloud backup is optional — not required to use the app. Add it later if you want cross-device access.

How data is stored

All projects, captures, voice notes, and report drafts are stored in a local SQLite database on the device. Photos are stored in the app's private file storage — separate from your camera roll, secure, and unaffected by your camera roll settings.

Nothing leaves the device unless you explicitly share a file. There is no background sync, no automatic upload, no cloud dependency in the core workflow.

Frequently asked questions

Does CapturePort need any internet connection at all?

No. The app works completely offline. The only thing that requires connectivity is creating your account when you first export a report (one-time, via email OTP) and optionally sharing the exported file. All capturing, drafting, and exporting happens locally on the device.

What happens to my data if I lose the device?

Data lives on the device. If you lose the device without a backup, the data is lost. Cloud backup — which syncs your projects to secure cloud storage — is on the product roadmap as an optional paid feature. Until then, consider enabling your device's local backup (Google One or equivalent).

How large are the exported report files?

Compact quality PDFs with 20 photos are typically 3–6MB. High quality exports with 60 photos can reach 30–50MB. Files are stored locally and shared via your choice of app — email, WhatsApp, cloud drive, or any sharing method your device supports.
