Marine Reporting App for Engineers and Surveyors

CapturePort is the offline-first mobile app built for marine service engineers, surveyors, attendance officers, and offshore teams who need to produce professional vessel reports without wasting time on paperwork after they leave the job.

Built around marine reporting realities

Marine inspections and service attendances happen on vessels, in engine rooms, at anchorage, in dry-dock, and at offshore facilities — environments where connectivity is unreliable, conditions are challenging, and the pressure to produce a clean report quickly is real. CapturePort is designed specifically for this.

  • Fully offline — works in engine rooms, cargo holds, and remote anchorages with no signal
  • Photo capture with instant captioning — caption each photo before you move to the next area, not hours later from memory
  • Voice notes — record spoken observations when your hands are occupied or conditions make typing impractical
  • PDF and Word export — generate a clean professional report before you leave the vessel or port
  • No login required — start capturing immediately, no account setup on site

The field reporting problem: Most marine professionals leave the vessel with a phone full of uncaptioned photos and incomplete notes, then spend two hours writing the report from memory that evening. CapturePort eliminates that gap — you capture, caption, and generate the draft on board.

Frequently asked questions

Does CapturePort work without mobile data on board a vessel?

Yes. CapturePort is fully offline-first. All photos, voice notes, and report drafts are saved to your device. No internet connection is needed at any point to capture or generate reports. You can export the final PDF or Word file and share it when you are back in a connected area.

Can I produce reports in the format my client expects?

CapturePort generates structured reports from your captures. In the current version, you can add company branding, report identity, and commentary. Custom client letterhead and format matching are on the roadmap. The exported Word file is fully editable so you can adjust formatting as needed.

How do I handle a large number of photos in a vessel inspection report?

CapturePort lets you reorder photos, caption each one on site, and control which captures are included in the report. You can choose how many photos appear per page in the export. Reports with 20–60 photos are common for vessel inspections and work cleanly with the current export.

Is CapturePort suitable for use by a marine survey company with multiple surveyors?

In the current version, each user works independently on their own device. Team accounts with shared project visibility and company branding are on the product roadmap. Join the pilot to be among the first to access team features when they are released.
