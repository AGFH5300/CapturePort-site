Marine Reporting App for Engineers and Service Teams

CapturePort is designed for marine service engineers, inspectors, repair teams, offshore technicians, and surveyors that prepare recurring technical reports onboard vessels and at marine facilities.

Marine inspections Service reports Offline-first Photo reporting

Designed around marine workflows

Marine reporting environments are different from normal office workflows. Teams often work onboard vessels, at anchor, in drydock, offshore, or inside engine rooms where quick documentation and structured reporting are critical.

CapturePort direction:

CapturePort focuses on helping technical teams capture field evidence quickly and convert raw observations into cleaner structured report drafts.

Common use cases

Vessel inspectionsPrepare onboard findings and condition reports with photos and notes.
Repair attendance reportsTrack repairs, observations, and completed service work.
Marine survey workflowsOrganize technical findings and structured reporting for surveys.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CapturePort suitable for marine engineers?

Yes. CapturePort is being positioned around field reporting workflows used by marine engineers, inspectors, repair teams, and offshore service providers.

Can reports include photos and notes?

CapturePort supports structured reporting workflows using photos, notes, voice input, and AI-assisted report drafting.

Can users review AI-generated reports?

Yes. All AI-generated report drafts must be reviewed and approved by users before external use.
