Account Management

Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your online CapturePort account and data associated with that account. Locally stored reports and copies already exported to your phone may require separate deletion.

⚠ Permanent action. Deleting your online account is permanent, but does not necessarily delete projects, photographs or exports stored only on your phone, in your Gallery, or with third parties. Back up the reports you need and remove local files separately if you wish.

Step-by-step

How to request account deletion

  • 1
    Export any reports you need Open CapturePort, go to each project and export any PDF or Word reports you want to keep. Account deletion may limit future account access; independently stored local files may still exist.
  • 2
    Email us from your registered address Send your request to support@captureport.app with the subject: "Account Deletion Request". Include your company name if applicable.
  • 3
    We confirm within 2 business days We'll send a confirmation email acknowledging your request and the expected deletion date.
  • 4
    Deletion completed within 30 days We aim to delete the online account and associated account data within 30 days after a verified request, except where limited retention is required by applicable law or legitimate security obligations. We will confirm completion. Local files on your device are managed separately.
Data handling

What gets deleted and what's retained

Data typeWhat happensWhere
Online account, email and verification recordsDelete upon verified requestAuthentication services, normally within 30 days unless lawful retention applies
Locally stored projects, photos, audio and draftsRemove separately in the app or on your deviceYour phone; not normally accessible for remote deletion
Gallery copies and separately exported PDF/Word filesRemove from their saved location if desiredDevice folders, Gallery or external storage
Copies already shared with third partiesOutside CapturePort's controlRecipient apps, messages or systems
Records retained by legal obligation or to address abuseLimited retention where necessaryOnly for the applicable purpose and retention period
