Account Management

Delete Your Account

Request permanent deletion of your CapturePort account and all associated data. This action cannot be undone.

⚠ Permanent action. Once your account is deleted, all your projects, inspection reports, photos, and exported files stored in CapturePort will be permanently removed. Please export any reports you wish to keep before submitting your request.

Step-by-step

How to request account deletion

  • 1
    Export any reports you need Open CapturePort, go to each project and export any PDF or Word reports you want to keep. Once deleted, these cannot be recovered.
  • 2
    Email us from your registered address Send your request to support@captureport.app with the subject: "Account Deletion Request". Include your company name if applicable.
  • 3
    We confirm within 2 business days We'll send a confirmation email acknowledging your request and the expected deletion date.
  • 4
    Deletion completed within 30 days Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted within 30 days of your confirmed request. You'll receive a final confirmation when complete.
