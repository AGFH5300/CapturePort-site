AI-assisted reporting roadmap in CapturePort

Most "AI report generators" ask you to describe what happened and then write a report for you. That is not how professional inspection reporting works — the accuracy of a field report depends on direct observation, not on a model's best guess.

CapturePort takes a different approach: you capture the evidence in the field (photos, voice notes, typed observations), and the AI assists with structuring and drafting the commentary around that evidence. The result is a report draft that reflects what you actually observed — not a fabricated summary.

Important: All AI-generated content in CapturePort is clearly marked as a draft. The user reviews and edits before any report is exported or shared. AI outputs are never used as final reports without human review. This is non-negotiable for professional inspection reporting.

What is implemented today

The current version of CapturePort uses structured industry templates to generate report drafts from your field captures. You select the report type, and the app generates a structured draft with appropriate section headings, executive summary prompts, and commentary placeholders matched to that report type.

AI features on the roadmap Coming soon

The following AI capabilities are in active development and will be added in future releases:

  • Voice transcription — automatically transcribe voice notes into text for inclusion in the report draft
  • AI commentary polish — improve grammar, clarity, and professional tone of your draft while preserving your observations
  • Photo caption suggestions — AI-suggested captions for photos based on image content
  • Summary generation — auto-generate an executive summary from your structured findings
  • Multi-language support — draft reports in Arabic, Hindi, Tagalog, and other languages from English observations

On AI and professional reports: CapturePort AI features are assistive, not autonomous. In regulated industries — marine survey, insurance inspection, construction — the professional's judgement and signature are what make a report valid. AI speeds up the drafting process; the professional makes it authoritative.

Frequently asked questions

Does CapturePort use AI to write my inspection report for me?

No. CapturePort uses structured templates to generate a report framework from your captures. You fill in the findings and review the draft before exporting. The goal is to eliminate the blank-page problem — not to replace the professional judgement that makes an inspection report credible.

Can AI-generated reports be used for insurance or class submissions?

Any report submitted for insurance, class, or regulatory purposes must be reviewed, edited, and signed by the responsible qualified professional. CapturePort generates drafts only. The professional remains responsible for accuracy, scope statements, and all factual content in the final report.

When will voice transcription be available?

Voice transcription is on the near-term product roadmap. Join the pilot program to be among the first to access it when released and to provide feedback on how it should work for your specific reporting workflow.
