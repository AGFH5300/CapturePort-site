Photo Report App for Field Teams

CapturePort turns your field photos into professional inspection and service reports — with numbered, captioned photos, structured findings, and clean export — directly from your phone, without needing an internet connection.

The photo reporting problem

Field engineers, inspectors, and service technicians typically leave a job site with 30–80 photos on their phone. Those photos are the core evidence in any inspection or service report. But most reporting workflows break down immediately after capture:

  • Photos are taken without captions and become meaningless hours later
  • Photos sit in a gallery with no connection to the written findings
  • Organising and numbering 50 photos in Word takes 40+ minutes
  • The final report is written from memory, not from the evidence

CapturePort solves this at the point of capture — not at the desk afterwards.

How CapturePort handles photos

1

Take or import photos on site

Use the in-app camera or import from your gallery. Photos are copied into secure local storage on your device.

2

Caption immediately after each photo

Add a caption the moment you take the photo — while you are still at the location. Use voice dictation on your keyboard for hands-free captioning.

3

Reorder photos to match your report structure

Move photos into the right order before you export. Use step buttons for adjacent moves or jump any photo to a specific position instantly.

4

Export — photo appendix built automatically

CapturePort generates a numbered photo appendix from your captioned, ordered photos. No manual work in Word or PDF. Export as PDF (final) or Word (editable).

Photo report features

  • Numbered photo appendix — photos automatically numbered (Photo 001, 002…) in the exported report
  • Captions preserved — every caption you added on site appears under the correct photo in the export
  • Photo reordering — drag any photo to any position before exporting

Frequently asked questions

Does CapturePort work with photos already in my gallery?

Yes. You can import photos from your phone gallery directly into any project. They are copied into the app's secure local storage and treated the same as photos taken with the in-app camera.

How many photos can a report contain?

There is no hard limit. Reports with 60–100 photos are common for vessel and construction inspections. The app handles large photo sets cleanly. Export time increases with photo count and selected image quality.

Are my original photos affected?

No. CapturePort copies photos into its own storage. Your original photos in the gallery or camera roll are never modified, moved, or deleted.

Can I control the image quality and file size of the exported report?

Yes. CapturePort offers compact, standard, and high image quality settings for exports. Compact produces a smaller file suitable for emailing. High quality preserves maximum detail for insurance or class submissions.
