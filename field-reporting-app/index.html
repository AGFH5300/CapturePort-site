Field Reporting App for Engineers, Inspectors, and Service Teams

CapturePort is the mobile-first, offline-first reporting app that helps field professionals capture evidence on site and generate professional reports before they leave. No internet connection required at any stage.

The field reporting problem

Field engineers, inspectors, and service technicians typically leave a job site or vessel with dozens of photos, handwritten notes, and a rough idea of what happened — and then spend one to three hours writing the report from memory back at the office. The result is a weaker report than the job deserved, and time wasted that could be spent on the next job.

CapturePort solves this at the source: you capture and caption evidence while you are still at the location, and the app generates a structured draft that is 70% written by the time you leave.

The core insight: A photo captioned on site in 15 seconds is worth more than a photo described from memory 4 hours later. CapturePort makes on-site captioning the default, not an afterthought.

Core features

📷 Photo capture with instant captions

Take or import photos and caption them immediately. Captions are saved to the photo and appear automatically in the exported report.

🎙 Voice notes — auto-labelled

Record spoken observations. Labels are automatically suggested from context — matched to the current photo or sequentially numbered.

📋 Typed notes and project details

Add project metadata, client name, vessel/site, job reference, and typed observations — all saved offline.

📄 PDF and Word export

Generate PDF (final, polished) or Word (editable draft) reports directly from your phone. Photo appendix built automatically.

🔃 Photo reorder and review

Reorder photos before exporting. Move any photo to any position with step controls or a direct position jump.

✈️ Fully offline

Engine rooms, remote sites, vessels at anchorage, underground facilities — CapturePort works everywhere with no connectivity needed.

How field reporting works with CapturePort

  1. Create a project — name the job, add vessel or site details, client, and reference number
  2. Capture on site — photos with captions, voice notes, and typed observations saved locally
  3. Review captures — swipe through, edit captions, delete anything that shouldn't be in the report
  4. Prepare report — select a template, reorder photos, add commentary
  5. Export and share — PDF or Word, straight to email, WhatsApp, or any app on your phone

Frequently asked questions

Does CapturePort work without internet?

Yes, completely. CapturePort is offline-first by design. Photos, voice notes, typed observations, and report drafts are all saved to your device. You can export a PDF or Word report without any internet connection. Files are shared when you choose to share them.

Do I need to create an account to use CapturePort?

No account or login is required in the current version. Download the app and start capturing immediately. Cloud sync and team accounts are on the roadmap for a future release.

What does the exported report look like?

The PDF export produces a formatted report with a header, project metadata table, commentary sections, and a photo appendix with numbered, captioned photos. The Word export is fully editable. You can see sample output by joining the pilot program and generating a test report.

Can I use CapturePort for a team?

In the current version, each user works independently on their own device. Team features with shared projects, company branding, and a web dashboard are on the product roadmap. Join the pilot to be first to access them.
