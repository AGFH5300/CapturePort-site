How it works

From job site to report in minutes

Caption photos as you take them. Record voice observations. Then export a professional report before you leave — all offline.

1

Create a project

Name the job, add vessel or site details, client, and job reference. Takes 20 seconds.

2

Capture on site

Photos with captions, voice notes, and typed observations — saved locally on your device. No internet needed.

3

Review captures

Swipe through your captures, edit captions, reorder photos. Get the sequence right before exporting.

4

Export and share

PDF (polished, print-ready) or Word (editable). Photo appendix built automatically. Share via email, WhatsApp, or any app.

Features

Built for real field conditions

✈️ Fully offline

Works in engine rooms, vessels at anchorage, remote sites, and underground facilities. No signal needed at any stage.

📷 Photo capture with instant captions

Caption each photo before you move to the next area — not hours later from memory.

🎙 Voice notes

Record spoken observations. Auto-labelled from context — matched to the current photo or sequentially numbered.

🔃 Photo reorder

Move photos into the right order before exporting. Step controls or direct position jump.

📄 PDF and Word export

PDF is polished and print-ready. Word is fully editable — ideal for supervisors who need to review or format before sending to the client.

🔒 Local storage

Reports live on your device. Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it. Cloud backup is an optional future upgrade.

📋 10 report templates

Marine service, vessel attendance, inspection, damage/defect, equipment inspection, maintenance, and more.

🚀 No login required

Download and start capturing immediately. No account setup on site.
