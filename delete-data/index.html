Data Control

Delete My Data

Request deletion of specific data from your CapturePort account — without closing your account. You stay in control of what's kept and what's removed.

Your account stays active. This page covers selective data deletion. If you want to permanently close your account and remove everything, visit the Delete Account page instead.

What you can request to delete

Types of data eligible for deletion

Selective

Projects & reports

One or more named projects, inspection reports, or captured items.

Selective

Photos & media

Specific photos or all media from a project.

Selective

Company branding

Your uploaded logo or letterhead template.

Full wipe

All project data

All projects and reports — keeping only your account profile active.

Process

How to submit a data deletion request

  • 1
    Identify the data you want removed Note the project name(s), report title(s), or data category. The more specific, the faster we can process it.
  • 2
    Email us from your registered address Send your request to support@captureport.app with the subject: "Data Deletion Request". Describe what you want removed.
  • 3
    We confirm within 2 business days We'll acknowledge your request and confirm which data will be deleted and when.
  • 4
    Deletion completed within 30 days Requested data is permanently removed within 30 days. You'll receive a confirmation once complete. Your account remains active throughout.
